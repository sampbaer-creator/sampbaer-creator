## BridgeWay

[**BridgeWay**](https://bridgewayrefer.com/) helps businesses track and follow up on referrals. A partner shares an NFC card or QR code, the customer fills out a short form, and the referral lands in the business’s dashboard with the referring partner and receiving office attached. Teams can manage cards, partners, locations, staff access, referral status, and subscriptions in the same workspace.

**Built with** Next.js, React, C#, ASP.NET Core, Azure Cosmos DB, Microsoft Entra, and Stripe.
